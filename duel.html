The rules

Fixed in the contract

The duel contract has no owner and no admin. Nobody can change these numbers, pause it or take a fee.

10 DRAW

Smallest bet

There is no largest. Both sides always bet the same amount.

1.8x

The payout

The pot is both bets. The winner receives 90% of it, which is 1.8 times the bet. The other 10% burns and DRAW's supply shrinks.

3 blocks

Who wins

The result comes from the hashes of the three blocks after the challenge is taken. Nobody knows them when it is taken. An odd seed means the challenger wins, an even seed means the taker wins.seed = keccak256(hash(N+1), hash(N+2), hash(N+3), id, challenger, taker)

8 seconds

Settling

Anyone can settle a duel once its three blocks exist. Our keeper does it within seconds. Settling only pays out a result that is already fixed.

Cancel

Changing your mind

You can cancel your challenge and get the whole bet back while nobody has taken it. Once it is taken, nobody can cancel it.

4.5 hours

Void

If nobody settles a duel for about 4.5 hours, its block hashes expire. The duel is then void and both players get their bet back in full.

Your hand

Betting moves DRAW out of your wallet

Your hand on the street shrinks by the bet. At 5 DRAW or less it leaves the street, and ETH it won but did not claim passes to the other hands. Claim your ETH on the street page first. A win sends the payout back to your wallet and your hand grows again.

Trust

Who could tilt a result

No player, keeper or team member can. Base's sequencer builds every block, so in theory it could shape the last of the three blocks. Mixing three blocks means no single earlier block decides anything.

Approve

Two wallet prompts

The first time you bet, your wallet asks you to let the duel contract move that amount of DRAW, then to post or take the challenge. Never send DRAW straight to the contract. It only tracks bets made here and cannot return anything else.
